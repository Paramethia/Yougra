How to get an album link?

Mobile

Go to the album you wanna download on YouTube music, tap the share icon, and choose to copy. There you go. Now you have the link.

Computer

While scrolling looking at a list of albums on YouTube music on PC, just click those 3 dots and you should see an option to share and copy the link.

Download method

Immediate

This ensures that every video you download always start immediatly without processing. Keep in mind that the file may be corrupted for some devices.

Process

This ensures that the video works on any device by having a process of downloading and merging on the server, then the download will start. Not recommended for long videos if you are inpatient, as it will take a long time before it starts the download. There is also a max file size requirement (5GB) for downloading videos for this option.

What is Yougra about?

Yougra is a web app that allows you to download videos, audio, songs, playlists, albums, and more. Simply search for what you want above and click the media you want to download as either video or audio. Once you select a video, you will be able to choose from different video quality options before downloading.

Help

In addition to searching, you can paste the exact YouTube video or song link along with playlists or album links to quickly grab them. Playlists are currently downloadable as audio only. If you're unsure how to get an album link, you can find instructions in the Settings.

Pro tip: Add "album" next to album name when searching, to easily find actual albums instead of playlists pretending to be albums.

Speaking of settings, you will also find download methods there for videos. There are two methods: Immediate and Process. By default, the Process method is selected, but you can switch between them at any time. The differences between these methods are explained in the Settings menu.

Features

  • Audio/Music detection system (auto selects fitting format)
  • Video quality selection choice
  • Download audio as m4a or mp3
  • Download audio from YT video
  • Custom download methods
  • Content link sharing (share links to videos/songs or playlists/albums for friends to download)
  • Custom search results (coming soon)
  • Custom audio metadata (coming soon)
  • Download stats (coming eventually)
